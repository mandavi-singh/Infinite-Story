# Infinite Story – Presentation Slide Content

## Slide 1 — Infinite Story
**An AI-Powered Fantasy Adventure**  
Python • Generative AI • Interactive Storytelling

## Slide 2 — Project Idea
- A console-based fantasy and mystery adventure.
- The player shapes the story through decisions.
- AI generates scenes, consequences, feedback, and the final chapter.
- Each playthrough can develop differently.

## Slide 3 — Explore Three Worlds
- **Enchanted Forest:** Investigate a magical forest mystery.
- **Lost Kingdom:** Uncover secrets of a vanished kingdom.
- **Mystery Island:** Search for the missing Heart of the Island.

## Slide 4 — How It Works
1. Enter character name and select a world.
2. AI creates an opening scene with three choices.
3. Player completes six decision rounds.
4. Story memory carries previous events and choices forward.
5. AI generates the final chapter and ending.

## Slide 5 — AI Integration
Uses `from ai import call_gpt` in the Code in Place environment.
- **Scene generation:** Creates context-aware story scenes and choices.
- **Decision evaluation:** Returns points (0–15), category, and feedback.
- **Ending generation:** Selects Hero, Secret, Sacrifice, or Mystery.

## Slide 6 — Scoring & Achievements
- Six decisions; maximum possible score: 90 points.
- AI evaluates choices in the context of the story.
- Categories include clue, help, strategy, risk, exploration, and other.
- Achievements reward completion, variety, score, categories, and ending type.

## Slide 7 — Technologies & Responsible AI
- Python for game logic, input handling, memory, scoring, and achievements.
- Code in Place AI environment for generative AI calls.
- Designed for age-appropriate fantasy and mystery content.
- No sensitive personal information is required.

## Slide 8 — Limitations & Future Scope
- Current version is console-based.
- No persistent save/load feature yet.
- AI feedback is subjective and may occasionally misclassify a decision.
- Future: GUI, saved adventures, inventory, maps, more worlds, richer branching.

## Slide 9 — Live Demo & Conclusion
- Enter a character name and choose a world.
- Play a few rounds and show how choices affect the next scene.
- Show the final ending, scorecard, and achievements.
- Infinite Story demonstrates how Python and generative AI can create interactive storytelling.
